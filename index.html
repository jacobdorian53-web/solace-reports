📌 Q1 Headline

Solace closed Q1 at $8.71M DTC revenue and $9.95M combined with Amazon, shipping 120K orders and acquiring 90K new customers. Combined Net Profit hit $1.06M across DTC + Amazon (10.6% combined margin).

Q1 blended efficiency: 1.68x ROAS, 1.30x NC ROAS, $57.44 NC CPA. The arc is clear — Jan over-spent for volume, Feb pulled back hard to prove the profitability case, Mar scaled back up while *holding* efficiency. NC CPA tightened from $62.90 → $59.60 → $50.90 across the quarter.

Channel concentration: Meta 75% of paid spend, with Google at 12% the second pillar. The other three (TikTok, Applovin, Pinterest) combined are ~13% — Applovin earned its keep in March and is the most likely scale candidate heading into Q2.

📋 Overview

The Big Picture — March 2026

March was a scale month with held efficiency. Order revenue grew 36% MoM ($2.33M → $3.17M) on a 38% spend increase ($1.32M → $1.82M), so blended ROAS held at 1.74x (vs 1.77x in Feb — basically flat). The real wins were on acquisition cost: blended CPA dropped 9.9% to $41.01 and NC CPA dropped 14.6% to $50.90. Net Customer ROAS ticked up to 1.36x (+2.3%).

Direct-side net profit grew to $231K (+15.7%) on a 7% margin (down 2pp as spend ramped). The headline number: Amazon revenue tripled to $705K (+181%), pushing combined Net Profit + Amazon to $531K (+69.9%). Combined order revenue including Amazon hit $3.84M (+48.6%).

🏆 March Channel Winners

  • Meta — Big efficiency unlock. Spend +45% but TW ROAS climbed to 1.63x (+26.4%), NC CPA down 23.3% to $59.54, Cost per New Visitor cut 35.5% to $1.71. Quality and volume both up.
  • Applovin — Quietly the best month. Every efficiency metric improved: ROAS 1.93x (+0.08), NC ROAS 1.36x (+11.5%), NC CPA -17.8%, Cost NV -40.6%, NV% +7.4pp. Strong scale candidate.
  • Google — Took the volume role. Spend +54% to $236K, revenue +36%. ROAS softened to 2.85x (still the highest blended ROAS channel). Per-acquisition cost held flat (CPA -0.1%), but AOV/NV% slipped — signals lower-quality traffic mix as it scaled.
  • Pinterest — Trajectory still healing on small dollars. ROAS 0.99x (+20.7%), NC CPA -30.9% to $89.98. Worth keeping the test live.

⚠️ Watchouts

  • TikTok — Still shrinking. Spend -14.7%, ROAS slipped to 1.26x, NC ROAS stuck at 0.36x. Channel buys repeat customers, not new ones. Decide: cut deeper or pivot creative strategy.
  • Net Margin — Dropped 2pp to 7% as spend ramp outpaced revenue growth. MER ticked up to 58% (vs 56% in Feb). Watch this in April — current pacing model targets 1.74x MER, so we're at the line.
  • Returning Customer Mix — Returning revenue grew 17% but customer count grew 19.9% — implies returning AOV softened. Acquisition is healthy but retention engine deserves attention.

📈 3-Month Arc

January = volume, February = profitability discipline, March = controlled scale. The team proved in Feb that we could run lean and profitable, then in March pushed spend back up without giving up the efficiency gains — NC CPA actually improved further from $59.60 to $50.90 even at +38% spend. This is the right shape for a Q2 ramp.
